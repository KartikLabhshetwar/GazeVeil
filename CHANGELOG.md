# Changelog

All notable changes to GazeVeil are documented here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-20

### Added

- AirPods head-motion tracking with user-controlled centering and sensitivity.
- Direction-aware privacy shielding for left, right, upward, and downward movement.
- Native Liquid Glass on macOS 26 with a system-material fallback on macOS 14 and 15.
- Multi-display protection, click-to-clear behavior, and an eight-second failsafe.
- Menu bar controls for protection, recentering, shield preview, and settings.
- Guided onboarding for AirPods setup and the Reduce Transparency requirement.
- Signed and Apple-notarized downloads for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

### Privacy

- No camera access, screen capture, saved motion history, analytics, or network service.
